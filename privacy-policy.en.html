Privacy Policy — Manga Offline

Last updated: 2026-10-14

This document describes how the two editions of the Manga Offline app handle data:

  • Google Play edition — distributed exclusively through the Google Play Store.
  • Full edition — distributed outside Google Play (GitHub Releases), for Windows and Android.

1. Summary

The app does not collect, transmit or share personal data. There is no sign-up, login, user account, ads, trackers or analytics. The developer operates no server of its own: nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

2. Data that stays on your device

Your library, chapters, images, reading progress, preferences and settings are stored locally on your device, inside the app's private storage.

You can delete them at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in the system settings.

3. Google Play edition

3.1 Internet access

The internet is used only to fetch public metadata (title, cover, description, category) from the public AniList API (anilist.co). Only the title search term is sent — no personal identifier, no device identifier and no usage data.

3.2 No downloading of third-party content

The Google Play edition does not include third-party server search or content downloading. It works with files you already own.

3.3 Permissions

  • Internet — to fetch public metadata, as described in section 3.1.

No other sensitive permission is requested.

4. Full edition (outside Google Play)

4.1 User-configurable third-party sources

The full edition lets you search and download content from third-party sources that you configure yourself in the app settings. Those sources are operated by third parties, not by the developer, and have their own privacy policies and terms of use. Accessing them is subject to those third parties' terms and is your responsibility.

4.2 What is sent to those sources

When you use a source, the app sends that server only the request it needs (for example, the title of the work). The developer does not intermediate, log or have access to those requests.

4.3 Storage

Downloaded content and fetched metadata are stored locally on your device, under the same section 2.

4.4 Permissions

  • Internet — to query the third-party sources you configure.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent or share personal data. There is no personal data under our control to share.

6. Retention and deletion

We retain no data: nothing leaves your device. To delete everything, uninstall the app or clear its data.

7. Security

Data lives in the app's private storage, protected by the operating system sandbox.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children and collects data from no one, of any age.

9. Third-party content and copyright

The app is a file reader and organizer. It does not host, distribute or index content. We provide no content: the app only displays files you already own or that you choose to access from third-party sources under your own responsibility.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The date at the top is the last revision.

11. Contact

Questions, requests or complaints: github.com/brenosaran/Manga-Offline-Legal/issues
