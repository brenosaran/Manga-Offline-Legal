Privacy Policy — Manga Offline

Last updated: 2026-10-14

This policy describes how the Manga Offline app, distributed through the Google Play Store, handles data.

1. Summary

The app does not collect, transmit or share personal data. There is no sign-up, login, user account, ads, trackers or analytics. The developer operates no server of its own: nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

2. Data that stays on your device

Your library, chapters, images, reading progress, preferences and settings are stored locally on your device, inside the app's private storage.

You can delete them at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in the system settings.

3. Internet access

The internet is used only to fetch public metadata (title, cover, description, category) from the public AniList API (anilist.co). Only the title search term is sent — no personal identifier, no device identifier and no usage data.

4. No content downloads

The app does not include third-party server search or content downloading. It works with files you already own.

5. Permissions

  • Internet — to fetch public metadata, as described in section 3.

No other sensitive permission is requested.

6. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent or share personal data. There is no personal data under our control to share.

7. Retention and deletion

We retain no data: nothing leaves your device. To delete everything, uninstall the app or clear its data.

8. Security

Data lives in the app's private storage, protected by the operating system sandbox.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children and collects data from no one, of any age.

10. Content and copyright

The app is a file reader and organizer. It does not host, distribute or index content. We provide no content: the app only displays files you already own.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. The date at the top is the last revision.

12. Contact

Questions, requests or complaints: github.com/brenosaran/Manga-Offline-Legal/issues
